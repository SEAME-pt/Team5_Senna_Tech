## Description
This assertion verifies that the PiRacer’s speed sensor correctly measures and reports the vehicle’s actual speed.
The purpose is to ensure that the data provided by the sensor aligns closely with the real movement of the vehicle, enabling reliable controla and monitoring.

## Verification Criteria
- The sensor output shall update in a smooth way.
- In case of no vehicle movement, the sensor shall report 0km/h.
