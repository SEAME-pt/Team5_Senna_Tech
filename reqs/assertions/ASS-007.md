## Description
This assertion verifies that the system correctly reads and reports the remaining battery level of the Raspberry Pi 5 power supply or expansion board.
The purpose is to ensure that the retrieved battery information reflects the actual energy level available, allowing the system to monitor power status and trigger appropriate actions when the battery is low.

## Verification Criteria
- The system should read energy report from the INA219 sensor from the expansion board.
- The system shall successfully retrieve battery level data from the expansion board or designated power interface.
