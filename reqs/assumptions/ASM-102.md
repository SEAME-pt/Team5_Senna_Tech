id: ASM-102
related_assertion_id: AST-104
description:
  It is assumed that the transmission process on the AGL (Raspberry Pi 5) is configured with real-time priority (using chrt or SCHED_FIFO), ensuring that the Linux kernel prioritizes sending CAN frames every 2ms over other non-critical tasks.
  It is assumed that the clock drift between the STM32 oscillator and the Raspberry Pi 5 oscillator is less than 0.5% at operating temperature (0-70°C), allowing the jitter tolerance window (1.8ms to 2.2ms) to be maintained.
