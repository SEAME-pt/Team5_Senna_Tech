title: High-frequency temporal determinism
description: 
  The Raspberry Pi must transmit control messages at a constant frequency of 500 Hz. Given the short interval between messages (2ms), any delay in scheduling tasks in the AGL can cause packet loss or jitter, impacting the stability of high-speed control.
