# ⚡ Energy Management

This directory contains all documentation related to the project's energy management, including component power specifications, battery management, and power consumption analysis.

## 📑 Table of Contents
1. [Purpose](#1-purpose)
2. [Documents in this Directory](#2-documents-in-this-directory)

---

## 1. Purpose

The main purpose of this documentation is to provide a centralized location for all information regarding the power consumption of the project. This allows the team to:

-   Understand the power requirements of each component.
-   Properly size the battery and power supply.
-   Analyze and optimize the overall energy consumption of the system.
-   Document critical power limitations and their implications for hardware design.

## 2. Documents in this Directory

-   [**components_datasheet.md**](components_datasheet.md): A detailed list of all electronic components used in the project, along with their power consumption specifications (voltage, current, etc.), including critical notes on power limitations and their consequences for system stability.

-   [**spreadsheet_guide.md**](spreadsheet_guide.md): A guide on how to use the power consumption calculation spreadsheet. This spreadsheet helps in analyzing the total power consumption of the system under different operating conditions.

-   [**energy_consumption_analysis.md**](energy_consumption_analysis.md): A technical report detailing the comprehensive analysis of the system's power budget, critical component limitations, and the overall conclusion regarding the power architecture.

-   [**power_architecture_solution.md**](power_architecture_solution.md): The proposed new energy architecture, detailing the chosen components, power flow, and safety measures to resolve the identified power deficiencies.

## 3. How to Use

To effectively use the information in this directory, it is recommended to:

1.  Start by reading the [**components_datasheet.md**](components_datasheet.md) to understand the power requirements and critical limitations of each component.
2.  Use the information from the datasheet to fill in the power consumption calculation spreadsheet, as described in the [**spreadsheet_guide.md**](spreadsheet_guide.md).
3.  Review the [**energy_consumption_analysis.md**](energy_consumption_analysis.md) report for a comprehensive understanding of the system's power budget and the implications for hardware design.
4.  Analyze the results from the spreadsheet and the analysis report to make informed decisions about battery management and power supply design.
